हर दिन शास्त्रों से एक दिव्य कथा

DevGaatha tells one short story from Hindu scripture every day: Krishna's childhood, Bal Hanuman, Ram's Bal Kand, the Dashavatar, Shiva and Parvati, the Devi Mahatmya and the Upanishads.

Every story is retold in our own words and animated fresh for each episode. It is checked against the original text (Shrimad Bhagavatam, Valmiki Ramayana, the Puranas, the Upanishads), its source is named in every post, and anything from later tradition is marked on screen.
