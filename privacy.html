Privacy Policy

DevGaatha | देवगाथा · last updated 12 November 2026

Who this covers

This policy covers the DevGaatha publishing app (Google Cloud project "desktop-app-492200"). The app is a private tool used only by the owner of the DevGaatha channel to publish the channel's own videos. It is not offered to other users.

What the app accesses

  • YouTube (Google user data): with the channel owner's consent, the app uploads DevGaatha's videos to DevGaatha's own YouTube channel, sets their titles, descriptions, captions and audio-track languages, and reads the channel's own statistics and analytics (views, likes, watch time).
  • Instagram: the app publishes DevGaatha's Reels to DevGaatha's own Instagram account and reads that account's own insights.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect, store or process any data about viewers or any other person.
  • It does not access any account other than DevGaatha's own.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties, and does not use any data for advertising.

Storage and security

The login tokens the channel owner grants are stored only on the channel's private server, readable only by the publishing service, and are never published. The channel owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services User Data Policy

DevGaatha's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; see also the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
